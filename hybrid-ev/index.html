Servicing & Diagnostic Services

Hybrid & EV System Diagnostics

System fault investigation, scan-tool data interpretation, battery management and charging-system diagnostic assessment.

Electrical & Electronic Servicing

Electrical testing, wiring and harness fault tracing, sensor analysis, control modules and associated maintenance diagnostics.

Battery Health Assessment

Battery state-of-health evaluation, temperature and voltage data analysis and recommendations for suitable next steps.

Service capability and repair scope depend on vehicle model, equipment and safety assessment. High-voltage work requires appropriately qualified personnel, tools and protective equipment.

Education for technicians

Hybrid & EV Technical Training

Introductory sessions and practical workshops designed to build understanding of hybrid and EV architecture, battery chemistry, battery management systems, safe isolation principles, diagnostic scan data, thermal management and charging systems.

Training formats may include talks, demonstrations and supervised practical sessions depending on equipment availability.

Emerging Technologies

Our interests include second-life battery applications, stationary storage, solar-energy storage integration, backup power systems and responsible battery evaluation.
